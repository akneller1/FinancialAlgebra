5th Grade Math · Planning

Grade 5 Pacing Guide

A 180-day plan that teaches all eleven units, then reviews before the spring MCAS. Days are class periods of about 60 minutes. The MCAS testing window changes each year: check the Massachusetts DESE schedule and your district calendar, and move the review block to end the week before your test dates.

In each unit. Day 1: the unit’s flashcards and cheat sheet. Most days: a lesson from the Lesson Plan Generator at the level that fits each group (Support, Standard or Advanced). Middle of the unit: the Understanding Check. Last two days: a review game and an assessment from the Assessment Generator. Projects can run alongside, or after the MCAS.
If you fall behind. Units 1–6 (number, operations and fractions) carry most of the Grade 5 MCAS points. Shorten Units 9–11 by a few days rather than rushing fractions, and keep the review block.

Written for Mr. K’s Classroom. The day counts are a suggestion, not a requirement.
