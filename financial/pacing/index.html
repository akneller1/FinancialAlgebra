Pacing Guide and Standards Map

Ready-to-use plans for an 18-week half-credit course and a 36-week full-year course, and a map from every skill to the National Standards for Personal Financial Education (2021) from the Council for Economic Education and Jump$tart Coalition. Use it to show that your state's personal finance requirement is covered.

Every lesson day has a ready lesson plan in the unit's Lesson Plan generator, at Support, Standard and Advanced levels. Review days use the Practice Test, Error Analysis and games; assessment days use the Assessment generator. The semester plan teaches one lesson per skill; the full-year plan adds a practice and modeling day for each skill, a project in every unit, both days of each Spreadsheet Lab and a capstone.

Semester plan: 18 weeks, 90 class days (half credit)

One lesson per skill, every Decision Lab and Spreadsheet Lab, three projects (budgeting, credit and retirement), a midterm after Unit 5 and a final exam.
