Decimal Grid Lab

Use a hundredths grid to see why tenths times tenths makes hundredths, and why multiplying by a decimal less than 1 makes a smaller product. Use the interactive lab, or shade the models below.

  1. Shade 0.3 of the grid by coloring 3 columns. How many hundredths is that?
  2. Now color 0.4 of the rows. The part colored twice shows 0.4 × 0.3. How many small squares is it?
  3. Write the product 0.4 × 0.3 as a decimal.
  4. Is 0.4 × 0.3 greater than or less than 0.3? Why?
  5. Use the grid to find 0.6 × 0.5.
  6. Find 2 × 0.35 by shading 0.35 on two grids. What is the product?
  7. Without the grid: which is greater, 0.8 × 0.9 or 0.9?
  8. Write a multiplication of two decimals whose product is 0.24.
