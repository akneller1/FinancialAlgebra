Housing: Rent or Buy — Cheat Sheet

Financial Algebra · Unit 7: Housing: Rent or Buy

Essential vocabulary

Lease (rental)
A contract to rent a home for a set term at a set rent, with rules for both tenant and landlord
Security deposit
Money paid to a landlord up front to cover damage or unpaid rent, generally refundable
Renters insurance
Insurance that covers a tenant’s belongings and liability, not the building
Mortgage
A loan used to buy real estate, secured by the property
Principal and interest (P&I)
The portion of a mortgage payment that repays the loan and pays interest
PITI
Principal, interest, property taxes, and homeowners insurance — the full monthly housing payment
Down payment
The part of a home’s price paid in cash at purchase
Private mortgage insurance (PMI)
Insurance protecting the lender, typically required when the down payment is under 20% on a conventional loan
Closing costs
Fees paid to complete a home purchase, often 2–5% of the price
Property tax
A local tax on real estate based on its assessed value
Equity
The part of a home’s value that the owner truly owns: market value minus loan balance
Appreciation
An increase in the value of an asset over time
Opportunity cost (housing)
The return given up by tying cash up in a down payment and closing costs instead of investing it
Front-end ratio
Housing payment divided by gross monthly income; many lenders prefer about 28% or less
Debt-to-income ratio (DTI)
All monthly debt payments, including housing, divided by gross monthly income
HOA fee
A monthly fee paid to a homeowners association for shared maintenance and amenities
Maintenance reserve
Money set aside for home repairs; a common rule of thumb is 1–2% of home value per year

Common misconceptions

  • Renting is always a waste.
  • Homes always appreciate.
  • Mortgage payment = cost of owning.
  • Approval = affordability.

Worked example

  1. $350,000 home, 20% down → $280,000 loan at 6.75%, 30 yr
  2. P&I ≈ $1,816.07
  3. Tax 1.2% → $350/mo; insurance $1,500 → $125/mo
  4. PITI ≈ $2,291.07; + maintenance ≈ $291.67 → ≈ $2,582.74

Questions to ask before deciding

  • How long will I stay?
  • What is the full monthly cost (PITI + maintenance)?
  • How much cash do I need to close and still keep an emergency fund?
  • What could the down payment earn elsewhere?
  • What if prices fall?

Decision tips

  • Get preapproved and compare lenders.
  • Keep an emergency fund after closing.
  • Budget maintenance from day one.
  • Run optimistic and pessimistic scenarios.
