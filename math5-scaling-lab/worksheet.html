Use an area model to multiply fractions, then predict whether a product is greater or less than a factor before you compute. Use the interactive lab, or shade the models below.

  1. Shade 23 of the square with vertical lines and 34 with horizontal lines. What fraction is shaded twice?
  2. Write the multiplication the model shows.
  3. Is 23 × 34 greater or less than 34? Explain with the model.
  4. Predict without computing: is 5 × 78 greater or less than 5?
  5. Predict: is 5 × 114 greater or less than 5?
  6. Compute 5 × 78 and 5 × 114 to check.
  7. A rectangle is 35 m by 23 m. What is its area?
  8. Write a fraction you could multiply 12 by to get a product between 6 and 12.
